SOPKA Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 14, 2026

SOPKA is a cycling computer that records rides on the user's Android device. The app does not contain advertising, does not use product-analytics SDKs, and does not require an application account.

Data processed by the app

Depending on the features used and permissions granted, SOPKA processes:

  • precise location, route, speed, altitude and ride timestamps;
  • heart-rate, cycling power, wheel-speed and cadence readings from connected sensors;
  • sensor names and Bluetooth addresses, and a Xiaomi authentication key if you enter one, to reconnect to selected devices;
  • rider data entered by the user, such as weight, date of birth (used to calculate age) and measured maximum heart rate;
  • app preferences and saved ride history;
  • route-planning points, saved places, search history and downloaded map/address data;
  • app version, Android version and device model for local operation and optional local diagnostics.

Ride history, sensor settings and rider data are stored locally on the device. The app does not require a developer-operated account. Ride history, health readings and rider profiles are not uploaded to a developer cloud database. Public route reviews are a separate optional feature described below.

Location and sensor readings are used for ride metrics, training zones and alerts, and saved history. A ride started by you can continue recording with an ongoing notification while the screen is off or another app is open. Navigation started by you can also continue with an ongoing notification independently of ride recording. You can pause or finish these features in SOPKA. Rider profile values are used for training calculations.

External services

SOPKA may contact the following services when their related features are used:

  • MET Norway for temperature and weather forecasts on Overview. While that screen is visible and location access is granted and enabled, requests send coordinates rounded to 0.01 degrees and the device IP address. Exact GPS fixes, ride tracks, history and sensor data are not sent. The latest forecast, rounded area and settlement are cached in private storage excluded from backup. A usable hourly forecast may be reused for up to 24 hours, including after a restart or network failure. Expires and If-Modified-Since govern refreshes; requests stop when Overview is hidden. Without location permission the cached place is not shown;
  • Android's system geocoding service to determine the city, village or other settlement on Overview. It receives the same rounded coordinates (0.01 degrees), not a ride track. The system provider may use a network backend and process the request under its own terms. A single settlement name and rounded area remain only in process memory for up to a day; a different area is resolved again. If the system service is unavailable, no settlement is invented;
  • OpenFreeMap map and tile services to display maps and preload the nearby map during an active ride while the app is on screen. Network requests disclose the device IP address and the requested map area to the service. Maps in ride history and exported story images can also request tiles for the route being displayed;
  • OpenTopoData to obtain elevation data. The coordinates needed for the request and the device IP address are sent to that service only after explicit consent in Settings → Ride → Online elevation. This feature is off by default and can be disabled at any time;
  • BRouter (brouter.de) to calculate online routes and alternatives, including automatic recalculation during active navigation. Requests disclose the precise endpoints and intermediate points of the requested route, routing profile and IP address. Downloading routing data also discloses the requested geographic cells and IP address. When downloaded routing data covers the route, SOPKA tries local routing first; a local graph failure can fall back to online routing;
  • Overpass API (overpass-api.de) when downloading or updating the address index of a selected offline region. Requests disclose the selected region bounds and IP address. Offline address search uses the downloaded local index; typed search text is not sent to Overpass by this feature;
  • Photon (photon.komoot.io) for online address and place search. The service receives the typed query, requested language and IP address. When a current region can be determined on the device, an additional search includes its name to prefer regional results. The ordinary search still supplies results from elsewhere. Ordinary searches do not include device GPS coordinates. Only when you enable Nearby (the crosshair in the search field), the service also receives a focus point rounded to a roughly 1 km grid. Exact GPS stays on the device and is used locally to order results within 25 km. Turning Nearby off removes coordinates from subsequent search requests. Ride tracks, history and favorites are never included in search requests. Search uses Photon when internet access is available. If the network or service is unavailable, the app automatically searches downloaded addresses on the device. A cache of up to 32 searches stays only in process memory; responses may be reused for ten minutes. It is not written to disk and is lost when the process ends;
  • Your external browser when you explicitly choose Feedback. SOPKA opens only sopka.fun without adding message text, identifiers, coordinates, technical information, query parameters or attachments. The browser and website handle the normal website visit, including network information such as the IP address and HTTP headers, under their own settings and policies. Any information you independently submit on the website is not prepared or sent by the Android app.
  • Google Play to check for available updates of the app installed through Play, and to download and install updates when you choose to update. Update availability and delivery are handled by Google Play;
  • Google Play for an optional in-app rating and review after saved rides. The ride counter and request timing stay on the device; ride tracks, coordinates and sensor readings are not sent to the review API. If you choose to submit, Google Play receives your rating and optional review text over an encrypted connection and handles their publication under its own rules. You can delete the review through Google Play or your Google Account. Closing the prompt does not submit a review. See Google Play In-App Reviews data handling.
  • Earlier versions (versionCode 11 and below) use Google Play when you open development support and, where available, choose a support purchase. Starting with versionCode 12, SOPKA has no donation payments or Google Play Billing SDK. Google Play Billing queries the Play country, product prices and purchase status, and processes purchase tokens to complete the transaction. Google handles payment details; SOPKA does not receive your bank card number. There is no developer payment server in this version. Google Play Billing also includes a technical-event transport (Google DataTransport) that may send SDK diagnostics to Google. This SDK processing is separate from SOPKA's optional local diagnostics; SOPKA's diagnostics switch does not control it.

These services process data under their own privacy terms. SOPKA does not sell personal data or share it for advertising.

See OpenFreeMap privacy, MET Norway terms and privacy, OpenTopoData service information and Google privacy, BRouter privacy and Overpass API service information and Photon service information. Providers control their own retention; we do not promise that network requests are never logged.

In those earlier versions, Billing technical events can include app/SDK version information, Android version, device model and manufacturer, OS build information, language and region settings, time zone, network type and mobile operator codes. These are SDK service diagnostics, separate from the optional support ZIP prepared by SOPKA.

Android's Google Play services may use Location Accuracy to determine location. When enabled in Android settings, that Google service processes wireless signals, sensor/location information, IP address, device model and temporary rotating identifiers under Google's rules. See Location Accuracy. Android WebView may also report usage/crash diagnostics and pseudonymous report tokens when the user has enabled Google's corresponding system reporting. See WebView reporting. SOPKA's local diagnostics switch does not control these system services.

Optional public route reviews

Only when you press Send, SOPKA sends your chosen public nickname, one-to-five-star rating, optional comment, ride date and confirmation of route experience to SOPKA's route-review API on sopka.fun. A partial-route review is labelled in its text. The nickname, rating, date and review text are public on the website and in the app. Recorded GPS points, trip IDs, heart rate, rider profile, distances and workout durations are not included in this review request.

The app obtains and stores a signed anonymous visitor token to update the same review on retry or later editing. This identifier belongs to this installation; there is no login, password, email or phone-number requirement. The server receives normal network information, including an IP address. Draft reviews remain private on the device until you send them. Not now does not publish anything.

You can edit a sent review from the same installation. Public reviews are retained for display until removed by the operator; request removal at the support address, identifying the route and public review. Do not send your visitor token, sensor keys or GPS recording. Clearing app data removes its saved token and drafts, but does not itself remove an already published review. Support may need to verify that a removal request relates to your review. Reviews are not used for advertising.

Android backup

If Android backup is enabled, SOPKA allows only its non-sensitive appearance/theme preference to be backed up or transferred by Android. Rider profiles, personal training parameters, odometer data, ride history, routes, measurements, diagnostics, sensor pairing and keys are excluded. This applies to Android 8–8.1, Android 9–11, and Android 12 and later cloud backup and device transfer. The backup agent also applies the allowlist to other system transfer modes.

Excluded data cannot be automatically restored after reinstalling the app or transferring to another device. Existing local rides and settings are not deleted by this change. Manual ride-history archives and restore remain available; profiles and personal training settings are not added to those archives. Copies already held by an Android backup provider are not retroactively erased by changing the app rules.

Security and your choices

Ride history, preferences and authentication keys use private Android app storage. The Xiaomi key is stored in private preferences; SOPKA does not add separate encryption to those preferences. Online map and elevation requests use HTTPS. The Android sandbox restricts access by other apps; attachments use temporary read permission. Protection of exported files depends on the destination you choose.

You can revoke permissions in Android settings, turn off online elevation, and disable or delete diagnostics in SOPKA. Revoking location or Bluetooth limits those features. To delete all local settings and keys, clear app storage in Android settings. For access, correction or deletion of information sent to support, use the website contact form below. Support cannot remotely access or delete rides stored only on your device. No SOPKA account is created, so there is no app account to delete.

Retention and deletion

Data remains on the device until the user deletes rides, clears the app's data, or uninstalls the app. Android may retain a device backup according to the user's backup provider settings. The app also provides manual export and restore of ride history; exported files remain under the user's control.

Optional technical diagnostics are off by default. The local journal uses at most four files of 512 KiB (2.1 MB total), kept for no longer than seven days. Extended mode adds GPS accuracy and sensor communication timings for 24 hours. The journal is excluded from Android backups. You can disable and delete it in Settings → Privacy. No automatic upload occurs.

Only when you choose Send diagnostics and confirm, SOPKA sends a filtered technical journal, app version/build, Android API version, mode and a random request identifier to sopka.fun over HTTPS. The identifier makes retries refer to the same report; it is not a device or account identifier. The upload includes technical event timestamps, recording and sensor connection states, GPS/elevation/map processing and export results, exception classes and a limited stack. It excludes coordinates, routes, ride history, speed/heart-rate/power/cadence measurements, rider profiles, preferences, device names and Bluetooth addresses, authentication keys, passwords, email and full system/BLE logs. No email or application account is required. The server rechecks the allowed fields before storing a report and returns a report number only after saving it.

Reports are stored privately on the SOPKA VPS for 30 days from receipt. Expired reports become unavailable at 30 days; an hourly cleanup removes expired stored content even without new requests. Access to the report list, events and downloads requires the existing website administrator credential. A report number alone does not grant access. The diagnostics database is outside the public website directory and is not included in public site archives. The receiver does not store IP addresses in reports. As with any network request, website hosting and the receiving server process network addresses and HTTP metadata; hosting providers may retain ordinary infrastructure/security logs under their own terms. Uploads are used only to investigate technical problems and are not used for advertising or product analytics.

Feedback opens the external website. The Android app does not send email and does not attach ride archives to feedback. Information independently submitted through the website contact form is separate from technical reports; existing website correspondence is retained for up to 90 days after closure. Earlier Billing SDK processing described above remains independent of SOPKA diagnostics.

Sensor identifiers are removed from new manual history archives. Sensitive history files are excluded from automatic system backups and transfer. Older copies already exported or held by a backup provider are not retroactively changed.

Data deletion requests

To request deletion of information you sent to SOPKA support:

  • Use the contact form at sopka.fun and identify the support conversation or diagnostics report number. You may request early deletion before the 30-day report retention expires.
  • Do not attach ride files, sensor keys, passwords or new diagnostics to a deletion request. Support can remove the corresponding report and correspondence held by SOPKA; it cannot access local phone data remotely.
  • For a public route review, identify the route and public review in your request. Do not attach your visitor token or GPS recording. Removing the local draft or clearing app data does not delete a published review.

Support cannot remotely delete rides, health measurements, profiles or settings held only on your phone. Delete rides in History, delete diagnostics in Settings → Privacy, and use Android's app-storage settings to clear all local app data and saved keys. Exported files, Android backups and copies retained by your email or sharing provider remain under your or that provider's control; a request to SOPKA support does not remove those copies.

Permissions

Location is used to record rides and provide ride metrics. Bluetooth permissions are used only to discover and connect supported cycling and heart-rate sensors. Notification permission is used for active-ride status and alerts. Camera permission is requested only when you choose to scan a Xiaomi key QR code. The scan runs on the device; camera frames are not saved or uploaded.

Children

SOPKA is not directed to children under 13.

Changes to this policy

Updates are published on this page with a new effective date. Where a new use of sensitive data requires consent, the app must request it before that use.
